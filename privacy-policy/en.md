# Miqat al-Salat Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 28 October 2026**

This policy explains what the Miqat al-Salat app ("the app") does with your information. In short: **Miqat al-Salat does not collect,
store or share your personal data on any server. Everything the app knows stays on your phone.**

**Developer and contact:** Mounir BENHAJJAJ, miqatalsalat@gmail.com.

## 1. What the app does

Miqat al-Salat shows prayer times, the Qibla direction, remembrance texts (dhikr), a prayer-bead counter and the Quran with
translations. Prayer times and the Qibla are calculated on your phone from your location.

## 2. Information the app uses

| Information | Why | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **Your location** (approximate or precise), only if you choose "Use my current location" | To calculate prayer times and the Qibla for where you are | Used on your phone. The place you choose (a city, or a position from your phone) is saved in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to the developer. |
| **A place name**, only when you use your current location | To show "Casablanca" instead of coordinates | Your phone asks the Android location-name service (the system Geocoder, which on most phones is provided by Google) to turn the coordinates into a name. That request is made by the operating system, not by Miqat al-Salat, and is covered by the privacy policy of your phone's maker or Google. If no name is found, the app simply shows the coordinates. |
| **Your choices**: calculation method, language, theme, time format, prayer-time adjustments, notification options | To remember your settings | Saved on your phone only. |
| **Your progress**: your dhikr progress, your tasbih count, and your place in the Quran | To show your progress and reminders | Saved on your phone only. |
| **Your fasting log**: the days you marked as fasted, not fasted or made up, and the number of days you set to make up | To show your fasts and the days left to make up | Saved on your phone only. |
| **An audio file you choose** (optional), as your alert sound | To play your own sound for prayer alerts | A copy is kept in the app's private storage on your phone; it is not sent anywhere. You can remove it in Settings. |
| **A fasting log file** you export or import, only when you tap Export or Import | To keep your fasting log or move it to another phone | The file is written to, or read from, the place you pick in your phone's file chooser. The app does not send it anywhere. |
| **Notifications**: the permission to show prayer alerts | To remind you at prayer times | Alerts are scheduled and shown by your phone. Nothing is sent to a server. |

The app does **not** ask for or use: your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files (except the audio or fasting-log file you pick yourself, see above), microphone,
camera, calendar, advertising ID or any account. It has **no sign-in**, **no ads**, **no analytics** and **no crash
reporting**, and it does not include third-party tracking code. The app does not have the Android internet permission,
so it cannot send data over the internet by itself.

## 3. Location

Location is optional. You can pick a city from the list instead, and the app works fully without any location
permission. If you allow location, the app asks your phone for your position **only when you tap "Use my current
location"**, not in the background. You can allow "approximate" or "precise" location. You can turn the permission off at
any time in your phone's settings; the place you saved stays until you choose another.

## 4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone. The only data that leaves your phone is the place-name
request described in section 2, made by Android on your behalf.

## 5. Where the data is kept, and deleting it

Your information is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Android may include this private storage
(for example your fasting log) in your own device backup to your Google account if you have backups turned on; that backup
is under your control and your Google account settings. To delete everything, clear the app's data in your phone's
settings (Settings > Apps > Miqat al-Salat > Storage > Clear data) or uninstall the app. Because the developer holds no copy of
your data, there is nothing to delete on a server.

## 6. Content from other sources

The app includes the text of the Quran, translations published by QuranEnc.com (English by Noor International Center,
French by Rachid Maach) and the Scheherazade New font from SIL Global. This content is stored inside the app. The
"Sources and licenses" screen lists each source with its version. If you tap a web address there, your phone's browser
opens that site, which has its own privacy policy.

## 7. Children

Miqat al-Salat is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone,
including children.

## 8. Security

Because your data stays on your phone, its safety depends on your phone's own protection (screen lock, system updates).
The app does not transmit your data, which removes the main risk.

## 9. Your rights

Since the developer does not hold any personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on our side.
You control what is on your phone as described above. If you have a question about this policy, write to the contact
address at the top.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, if a feature would send data off your phone), this
page will be updated and the date at the top will change before that version is released.
